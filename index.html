About The Madrasa

A Sanctuary of Sacred Learning, Built for Girls

Madarsa Tus Salikat Lil Banat was founded with a singular conviction: that the Islamic education of girls deserves the same rigor, structure, and excellence historically reserved for institutional learning — delivered with complete privacy, dignity, and care.

For more than five years, we have served as an exclusively online, girls-only madrasa, welcoming students from across India and from Muslim communities around the world. Every class, every Ustadha, and every administrative process has been built around a single environment of safety: a space where mothers, daughters, and working women alike can pursue serious Islamic scholarship without compromise to their privacy or their daily responsibilities.

Our curriculum and rulings follow the Maslak of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah, specifically Maslak Aala Hazrat, and the fiqh of Imam Azam Abu Hanifa (R.A.). Classes are conducted live with experienced Ustadahs, supported by recorded lessons for revision and one-on-one personal guidance for every student's individual pace and circumstances.

We collect only the information needed to process admissions and respond to inquiries: name, age, contact number, and course preference.

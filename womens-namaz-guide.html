Complete Step-by-Step Namaz Guide for Women

A comprehensive, authentic guide for sisters and girls detailing the exact postures, rulings, and duas of Salah (Namaz) according to the Hanafi school of Islamic jurisprudence.

The Principle of Satr (Modesty) in Women's Prayer

In the sacred Islamic Shariah, the fundamental principle governing a woman's physical postures during Salah is Satr (maximum modesty and compactness). While the spiritual rewards, recitations, and obligations are equal for men and women, the Great Imams of Fiqh—including Imam-e-Azam Abu Hanifa and confirmed by Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (Aala Hazrat)—have established specific posture distinctions that safeguard a woman's natural dignity.

إِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ كَانَتْ عَلَى الْمُؤْمِنِينَ كِتَابًا مَّوْقُوتًا
"Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times." (Surah An-Nisa, 4:103)

Key Differences Between Men and Women in Namaz (Hanafi Fiqh)

The following summary highlights the primary physical distinctions every sister should observe:

Posture / Action Men (Mard) Women (Aurat)
Takbeer-e-Tehrima Raise hands up to earlobes, thumbs touching ears Raise hands up to shoulders inside sleeves
Hand Placement Fold hands below the navel, clasping the left wrist Place right palm over left hand on the chest (over breasts) without clasping
Ruku (Bowing) Bow fully straight with back level; spread fingers on knees Bow slightly so hands reach knees; keep fingers close together
Sajdah (Prostration) Keep elbows elevated, stomach away from thighs Keep elbows flat on floor, thighs drawn against the abdomen (compact)
Qa'dah (Sitting) Sit on left foot with right foot upright (Iftirash) Sit on left hip, extending both feet towards the right side (Tawarruk)
Voice / Recitation Can recite audibly in Jahri prayers (Fajr, Maghrib, Isha) Recite softly in all prayers (only audible to oneself)

1 Step 1: Pre-Requisites (Shurut) & Niyyah

  • Taharat (Purity): Perform Wudhu thoroughly. Ensure your clothing and prayer area are completely pure.
  • Satr (Dress Code): Cover the entire body with an opaque Abaya/cloth, including all hair, neck, and ankles. Only the face, hands, and feet may remain visible.
  • Facing Qiblah: Stand facing the Holy Ka'bah with feet positioned close together (approx. 2 to 4 inches apart).
  • The Niyyah: Make the intention in your heart (e.g. "I intend to pray 2 Rak'ats Fard of Fajr for the sake of Allah facing the Ka'bah").

2 Step 2: Takbeer, Qiyam & Recitation

  • Takbeer-e-Tehrima: Raise both hands inside your scarf/sleeves up to your shoulder level and say: "Allahu Akbar".
  • Hand Placement: Place your right hand over the back of your left hand resting flat on your chest.
  • Recite Sana (Subhanak Allahumma):
    سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ
    "Glory be to You, O Allah, and all praise is for You. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is none worthy of worship besides You."
  • Recite Ta'awwuz (A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir rajeem), Tasmiyah (Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem), followed by Surah Al-Fatiha and another Surah (e.g., Surah Al-Ikhlas).

3 Step 3: Ruku (Bowing) & Qawmah

  • Say "Allahu Akbar" and bow down into Ruku.
  • Form for Women: Bend forward just enough so your hands reach your knees. Keep your knees slightly bent and fingers joined together. Do not widen your elbows.
  • Recite the Ruku Tasbeeh 3 times:
    سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
    "Glory be to my Lord, the Most Magnificent."
  • Stand up straight (Qawmah) saying: "Sami' Allahu Liman Hamidah", followed by: "Rabbana Lakal Hamd".

4 Step 4: Sajdah (Prostration)

  • Say "Allahu Akbar" and prostrate in the most modest and compact manner:
    1. Place knees on the ground first, then hands, then nose, then forehead.
    2. Rest forearms and elbows completely flat on the floor.
    3. Keep your thighs pressed gently against your stomach.
    4. Point both feet outwards towards the right side.
  • Recite the Sajdah Tasbeeh 3 times:
    سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الأَعْلَى
    "Glory be to my Lord, the Most High."
  • Rise into a sitting position (Jalsah), pause briefly, then perform the second Sajdah in the identical compact manner.

5 Step 5: Qa'dah (Sitting) & Tashahhud

In the sitting posture (Tawarruk), sit directly on the floor resting on your left hip, with both feet drawn out towards the right side. Rest your hands flat on your thighs with fingers close together.

1. Recite At-Tahiyyat (Tashahhud):

التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَىٰ عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
"All compliments, prayers, and pure words are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is none worthy of worship but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger."

2. Recite Durood-e-Ibrahim:

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ، اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

3. Recite Dua-e-Masura:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي ظَلَمْتُ نَفْسِي ظُلْمًا كَثِيرًا وَلَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ فَاغْفِرْ لِي مَغْفِرَةً مِنْ عِنْدِكَ وَارْحَمْنِي إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ

4. Tasleem (Salam): Turn your face to the right saying "Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah", then to the left saying "Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah".

Dua-e-Qunoot for Witr Prayer

In the 3rd Rak'at of Witr (after Isha), after reciting Surah Al-Fatiha and a Surah, raise your hands up to your shoulders saying Allahu Akbar, fold them back on your chest, and recite Dua-e-Qunoot:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْتَعِينُكَ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَنُؤْمِنُ بِكَ وَنَتَوَكَّلُ عَلَيْكَ وَنُثْنِي عَلَيْكَ الْخَيْرَ وَنَشْكُرُكَ وَلَا نَكْفُرُكَ وَنَخْلَعُ وَنَتْرُكُ مَنْ يَفْجُرُكَ. اللَّهُمَّ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَلَكَ نُصَلِّي وَنَسْجُدُ وَإِلَيْكَ نَسْعَىٰ وَنَحْفِدُ وَنَرْجُو رَحْمَتَكَ وَنَخْشَىٰ عَذَابَكَ إِنَّ عَذَابَكَ بِالْكُفَّارِ مُلْحَقٌ
"O Allah! We seek Your assistance, ask for Your forgiveness, believe in You, put our trust in You, praise You in the best way, thank You and are not ungrateful to You, and we leave and cast off anyone who disobeys You. O Allah! You alone do we worship, to You we pray and prostrate, towards You we strive and hurry, we hope for Your mercy and fear Your punishment. Indeed, Your punishment will surely overtake the disbelievers."

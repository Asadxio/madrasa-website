1. The Quranic Obligation of Hijab & Parda

In Islam, Hijab is not merely a cultural tradition; it is a divine command revealed directly by Allah Almighty in the Holy Quran for the dignity, safety, and spiritual sanctity of Muslim women.

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ قُل لِّأَزْوَاجِكَ وَبَنَاتِكَ وَنِسَاءِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ يُدْنِينَ عَلَيْهِنَّ مِن جَلَابِيبِهِنَّ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ أَدْنَىٰ أَن يُعْرَفْنَ فَلَا يُؤْذَيْنَ

"O Prophet! Tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to draw their cloaks (Jilbabs) all over their bodies. That will be better, that they should be known (as respectable chaste women) so as not to be annoyed."

Surah Al-Ahzab (33:59) • Kanzul Iman Translation
وَقُل لِّلْمُؤْمِنَاتِ يَغْضُضْنَ مِنْ أَبْصَارِهِنَّ وَيَحْفَظْنَ فُرُوجَهُنَّ وَلَا يُبْدِينَ زِينَتَهُنَّ إِلَّا مَا ظَهَرَ مِنْهَا ۖ وَلْيَضْرِبْنَ بِخُمُرِهِنَّ عَلَىٰ جُيُوبِهِنَّ

"And tell the believing women to lower their gaze and guard their chastity, and not reveal their adornment except that which is apparent thereof, and to draw their head-covers (khumur) over their chests..."

Surah An-Nur (24:31)

3. Boundaries of a Woman's Awrah (Satr) in Hanafi Fiqh

According to Ala Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (R.A.) in Fatawa Razawiyyah and Allama Amjad Ali Azmi (R.A.) in Bahar-e-Shariat, the boundaries of Satr (parts of the body that must be concealed) vary depending on who is present:

Situation / In Front Of Required Coverage (Satr / Awrah) Fiqh Ruling & References
In Front of Non-Mahram Men
Outdoors, in public, or in the presence of ghair-mahram relatives (e.g. cousins, brother-in-law)		 Entire Body Including Face & Hair
Loose Jilbab / Abaya covering all hair, neck, ears, chest, body shape, and face (Niqab) in public environments to prevent fitnah.		 Fard (Obligatory)
Classical Hanafi consensus dictates that while the face is not intrinsically awrah, covering it in public is mandatory due to ubiquitous corruption (Fatawa Alamgiri, Durr-e-Mukhtar).
In Front of Mahram Men
Father, brother, son, paternal/maternal uncle, milk brother		 From Navel to Knee, Back & Belly
Exposing face, head hair, ears, neck, chest above collarbone, forearms up to elbow, and feet is permissible.		 Permissible
Only if there is complete safety and zero suspicion of fitnah (Bahar-e-Shariat, Vol 16).
In Front of Practicing Muslim Women
Mother, sisters, daughter, female friends		 From Navel to Knee
A woman cannot expose her body between the navel and knee in front of any other woman, even in private gatherings.		 Haram to Expose

Ghair-Muslim females		 Same as Non-Mahram Men

Based on Surah An-Nur: "Or their women" (أَوْ نِسَائِهِنَّ), referring specifically to Muslim women.

4. The 6 Conditions of a Valid Shar'i Hijab

1. Complete Coverage

It must cover all parts of the body designated as Awrah, including every strand of hair, the neck, and wrists.

2. Loose & Non-Form-Fitting

It must not be tight, skin-clinging, or outline the curves of the body, waist, or chest.

3. Thick & Opaque

The fabric must be thick enough so that skin color, shape, or undergarments are not visible through it.

4. Not an Adornment Itself

The outer cloak (Abaya) must not be ostentatious, overly glittering with bright sequins that attract gazes.

5. Not Perfumed Outside

The Prophet (ﷺ) strictly forbade women from applying strong scents when passing by non-mahram men.

6. Modest Demeanor

Hijab is an internal and external state: lowering the gaze, speaking without flirtatious softness, and walking with dignity.

5. Frequently Asked Questions on Parda (مسائل پردہ)

Is my male cousin (Chachazad, Mamazad bhai) a Mahram?

No, absolutely not. Cousins are strictly Non-Mahram. In Islam, marriage between cousins is fully valid, meaning full Shar'i Parda (covering hair, body, and modesty) is strictly compulsory in their presence.

Why is the brother-in-law (Dewar / Jeth) called "death" in Hadith?

Because family members let down their guard inside homes, opening the door to devastating sins and broken homes. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Beware of entering upon women." A companion asked: "What about the brother-in-law?" He replied: "The brother-in-law is death!" (Sahih Bukhari). Complete parda is mandatory from dewar and jeth.

Is a woman's voice part of Satr?

In Hanafi Fiqh, a woman's normal speaking voice is not Satr for necessities (such as asking for directions, buying groceries, or seeking knowledge). However, speaking with softness, melodiousness, or coquettishness in front of non-mahram men is strictly forbidden (Surah Al-Ahzab, 33:32).

At what age does Hijab become Fard for a girl?

Hijab becomes strictly obligatory (Fard) upon reaching the age of puberty (Buloogh). However, parents are commanded to train young daughters in modest dressing and scarves from the age of 9–10 so modesty becomes a natural part of their character.

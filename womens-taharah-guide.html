Women's Taharah, Haiz & Ghusl Guide

وَإِن كُنتُمْ جُنُبًا فَاطَّهَّرُوا ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ التَّوَّابِينَ وَيُحِبُّ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ

The authoritative Shar'i guide to ritual purification, the 3 obligatory acts of Ghusl, step-by-step Sunnah bath, rules for hair braids, and rulings on Haiz & Nifas according to Maslak Aala Hazrat (Bahar-e-Shariat).

1. The 3 Compulsory (Fard) Acts of Ghusl

If even one of these three acts is missed or done improperly, the Ghusl is **invalid (batil)**, and a person remains in a state of ritual impurity (Janabat/Hadath).

1
کلی کرنا (مضمضہ)

1. Rinsing the Mouth Thoroughly

Rinsing the mouth with water such that it reaches every single part of the oral cavity, from the lips to the base of the throat, between the teeth, and around dentures/fillings.

2
ناک میں پانی ڈالنا (استنشاق)

2. Sniffing Water into the Nostrils

Drawing clean water into both nostrils up to the soft bone (nasal cartilage), thoroughly cleansing any dried mucus or dirt that might prevent water from touching the skin.

3
پورے جسم پر پانی بہانا

3. Washing the Entire Body

Pouring water over the entire body from the crown of the head to the soles of the feet, ensuring that not even a single hair or millimeter of skin remains dry.

2. Complete Step-by-Step Method of Sunnah Ghusl

Follow this exact sunnah sequence when performing an obligatory or recommended bath:

1

Make Sincere Intention (Niyyah) & Wash Hands

Intend in your heart: "I intend to perform Ghusl to remove ritual impurity and attain purity for the sake of Allah." Wash both hands up to the wrists three times.

2

Wash Private Areas & Remove Any Physical Impurity

Wash the private parts thoroughly with the left hand, and cleanse away any traces of menstrual blood, vaginal discharge, or physical impurities (Najasat) from the body.

3

Perform Complete Wudu (Ablution)

Perform complete Wudu just as you do for Salah, taking extra care to gargle the mouth and draw water into the nostrils (Faraidh 1 & 2).

4

Wash the Head & Hair Roots (3 Times)

Pour water over your entire head three times, rubbing and massaging the scalp so that water reaches the roots of every hair strand.

5

Pour Water Over the Right Side (3 Times)

Pour water from the right shoulder down to the right foot three times, rubbing the body with your hands so no part remains dry.

6

Pour Water Over the Left Side (3 Times)

Pour water from the left shoulder down to the left foot three times, ensuring water reaches the navel, underarms, ear creases, and between the toes.

3. Critical Fiqh Rules for Women: Hair, Jewellery & Cosmetics

✓ What is Permissible / Exempt
  • Braided Hair (Gundi hui Chotiyan): If a woman has braided hair, it is NOT mandatory to untie the braids, provided water penetrates all the way to the roots and touches the entire scalp (Bahar-e-Shariat).
  • Natural Mehndi / Henna: Henna leaves a color stain and does NOT form a waterproof film; Wudu and Ghusl are 100% valid over henna.
  • Kohl (Surma) & Natural Oils: Oil or surma that does not form an impermeable layer does not prevent the validity of Ghusl.
  • Dhikr & Tasbeeh during Haiz: Reading Kalimah, Durood Sharif, and Istighfar during periods is permissible and rewarded.
✕ What Invalidates or Prevents Purity
  • Nail Polish / Gel / Enamel: Standard nail polish forms a waterproof plastic coating. Ghusl is INVALID until all polish is removed with acetone/remover.
  • Tight Rings & Nose Pins: Rings or nose pins (nath/koka) must be moved and rotated so that water flows beneath them. If dry skin remains under a ring, Ghusl is void.
  • Reciting Quranic Verses: Reciting verses of the Holy Quran with the tongue or touching the Quran without a separate detached cover is strictly forbidden during Haiz.
  • Fast / Salah: Offering Salah during periods is prohibited (no qada needed). Fasting is prohibited during periods, but missed Ramadan fasts MUST be made up (Qada) later.

4. Haiz, Nifas & Istihaza: Duration Standards (Hanafi Fiqh)

In the Hanafi Madhhab (Maslak Aala Hazrat), bleeding is classified into three distinct categories based on strict durations:

1. Haiz (حیض - Menstruation)

Minimum: Exactly 3 days and 3 nights (72 continuous hours).
Maximum: Exactly 10 days and 10 nights (240 hours).
Ruling: Prayer is excused; fasting is prohibited (qada required later). Ghusl is obligatory as soon as bleeding stops.

2. Nifas (نفاس - Postnatal Bleeding)

Minimum: No minimum limit (even 1 minute is valid).
Maximum: 40 days and 40 nights.
Ruling: Any bleeding beyond 40 days is considered Istihaza. Ghusl is obligatory once bleeding stops.

3. Istihaza (استحاضہ - Irregular Bleeding)

Definition: Bleeding less than 72 hours, or bleeding continuing past 10 days (beyond regular habit).
Ruling: It is an illness, NOT ritual impurity. The woman MUST pray and fast, making a fresh Wudu for each prayer time.

5. Frequently Asked Questions on Taharah (مسائل طہارت)

What should a sister do during prayer times when on her period?

It is highly recommended (Mustahabb) for a menstruating sister to make fresh Wudu at each prayer time, sit in her clean prayer area (Musalla), and spend 10–15 minutes reciting SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Astaghfirullah, and Durood Sharif so she does not lose her habit of worship.

Does dental work, fillings, or braces prevent the validity of Ghusl?

No. Dental braces, fixed fillings, and permanent crowns are deemed necessary medical fixtures (Haraj). Water flowing over them fulfills the requirement of Mazmaza (rinsing the mouth), and the Ghusl is fully valid in Hanafi Fiqh.

Can a woman touch or recite Islamic books containing Quranic verses?

A woman in Haiz cannot touch any Quranic text directly with bare hands. However, books of Tafseer, Hadith, or Fiqh where non-Quranic commentary exceeds the Quranic text may be touched by holding the non-Quranic borders, or using a detached cloth/pen.

What if a single hair root remained dry during Ghusl?

If a person remembers later that a specific spot (like behind the ear or under a ring) remained dry, they do NOT need to repeat the entire Ghusl. Simply washing that specific dry spot with water immediately completes the Ghusl!
